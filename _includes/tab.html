{%- comment -%} The logo tab: one SVG so the top rule and the tab read as a single shape (modeled on divider.html). The tab is the original outline (viewBox 2082.15 x 400, a 5.2:1 trapezoid with rounded lower corners). The rule is a stroked path that continues far past both edges; .site-tab clips it to the page width. Both take their fill from --tab-fill, set on .site-tab, so focus recolors them together (no hover effect on the shape). The logo is a mask over a background color (it is one flat color), so hovering the tab can tint it. Sizing is in components.css. On phones a plate (the wedge left of the tab's slanted edge, down to its flat bottom) squares off the tab's left side. It grows from its bottom-right apex along the slant, so the corner fills in as a wipe, not a fade. {%- endcomment -%}
